# HealthVerse — User Guide

## Getting Started

HealthVerse is a mobile-first healthcare platform. Open the app to see the splash screen, then proceed through login.

## User Types

### 🏥 Patient
- View nearby hospitals and doctors
- Book appointments (in-person or telemedicine)
- Track live hospital queue
- View health records and prescriptions
- Monitor medicines and refill schedules
- AI-powered health insights and risk scores
- Emergency SOS with ambulance dispatch
- AI symptom triage chatbot
- Blood and organ donation

### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor
- View patient queue with triage levels
- Access AI-generated patient summaries
- Write digital prescriptions
- Manage appointments

### 🚑 Ambulance Driver
- Receive emergency dispatch requests
- View patient location on map
- Accept/decline emergency calls
- Navigate to patient and hospital

## Key Features

### Emergency SOS
Tap the red SOS button on the bottom navigation to immediately dispatch the nearest ambulance.

### AI Triage
Answer the AI chatbot's questions to get an instant assessment of symptom severity (Green → Red).

### Appointments
Browse doctors by specialty, view available time slots, and book appointments with QR code confirmations.

### Health Records
View your complete medical history including blood reports, ECGs, X-rays, prescriptions, and vaccination records.

### Telemedicine
Start a video consultation with your doctor directly from the app.

## Theme

Toggle between Light and Dark mode in Settings.
